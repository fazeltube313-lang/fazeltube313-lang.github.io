# Chashm Desk - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Chashm Desk is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel it is connected to (Pooria Fazel, AWAKE EYE).

What it accesses: through the YouTube API Services (YouTube Data API v3), and only with the owner's permission, it reads and manages the owner's own channel data: videos, video details, playlists and comments.

How the data is used: only to show the owner his channel's comments and video details in his private Telegram chat, and to make the changes he requests, such as replying to or moderating comments and editing video details. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Storage and sharing: the access token and a small cache of recent items are stored only on the owner's private server. No data is sold or shared with third parties.

Revoking access: access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions

This tool uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service at https://www.youtube.com/t/terms and the Google Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy
