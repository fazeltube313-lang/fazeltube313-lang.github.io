# Chashm Desk

Chashm Desk is a private tool that Pooria Fazel (AWAKE EYE) uses to manage his own YouTube channel from Telegram: reading and replying to comments, and editing the titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails of his own videos.

It is not offered to the public. Only the channel owner uses it.
